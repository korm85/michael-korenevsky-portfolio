Build with Confidence: Ensure Quality and Throughput with AMVero using AI

Reduce scrap, increase throughput, and give your team a smarter, universal build process.

Product AMVero, On-Premise Author Michael Korenevsky, Senior PM

The Problem

In additive manufacturing, monitoring a diverse fleet of printers can be complex. AMVero changes that. It's a practical, straightforward monitoring tool that acts as an expert assistant for your entire team, giving you the power to prevent catastrophic errors, simplify quality control, and get part quality assurance from every machine, regardless of the manufacturer and the type of camera that is used.

AMVero's intelligent monitoring handles the routine observation of thousands of flawless layers, freeing operators to focus their expertise on higher-impact work like process optimization and quality assurance.

The AMVero Advantage: Intelligent, Simple, and Universal

We built AMVero to be different. Instead of locking you into a single ecosystem or requiring expensive, proprietary hardware, we provide a flexible solution that works for you. AMVero is a minimally invasive solution, meaning machines do not need to be requalified or validated.

  • No expensive hardware needed. Get started without a major capital investment. AMVero uses the in-situ monitoring cameras already widely available on industrial printers. There are no costly, proprietary sensors to install.
  • Intuitive from day one. Powerful software doesn't have to be complicated. AMVero features a clean, simple interface designed for a quick learning curve, so operators become experts in hours, not weeks.
  • Truly machine-agnostic. Designed for universal compatibility, AMVero supports all LPBF (Laser Powder Bed Fusion) 3D printers out of the box, freeing you from OEM lock-in. Manage your entire multi-brand fleet from a single, unified platform.

How AMVero Solves Your Daily Challenges

  • Detect and locate errors in real time. Our AI-powered system doesn't just find errors, it locates them, instantly detecting process-killing issues like warpage, spatter, recoater hopping, short feed, and recoater lines in the context of the as-built part. Operators get a simple, actionable alert that shows the exact location on the digital model to help them quickly evaluate severity.
  • Manage your entire fleet from a single dashboard. AMVero connects all your printers' critical manufacturing data into one clear dashboard. Get a high-level overview of your entire fleet on a single screen and instantly identify which machines require attention. Create alert definitions, receive notifications, and generate configurable reports at the end of each build.
  • Ensure full traceability. AMVero automatically logs all sensor data, imagery, and detected anomalies, creating a comprehensive digital record for each part: end-to-end traceability that is critical for quality assurance and certification.
  • Streamline root-cause analysis. Use historical build data for in-depth failure analysis. When a build fails, export the detailed information to analysis tools so engineers can investigate the root cause and prevent future occurrences.
  • Generate configurable, actionable reports. At the end of every job, AMVero automatically generates a comprehensive summary report. Configure reports to fit your quality standards, highlighting the most severe anomalies for each part, which significantly reduces manual review time.

Ensure Repeatable Quality with Build Compare

Achieve build-to-build consistency with the Build Compare tool. It enhances process control by enabling a direct, data-driven comparison between a current build and an approved "golden build." By automatically isolating and quantifying only the significant deviations, AMVero streamlines the pass/fail decision, eliminating tedious manual comparisons so you can approve parts with speed and confidence.

Trusted in Production by Industry Leaders

AMVero isn't just a concept; it's a production-proven solution. We are proud to partner with global energy technology leaders like Baker Hughes, who rely on AMVero in their demanding manufacturing environments to ensure part quality, reliability, and operational excellence.

  • Drastically cut scrap and rework costs. By catching critical errors as they occur, you stop wasting expensive powder and machine hours on parts destined to fail.
  • Save on costly post-processing. Enhanced in-process quality control and detailed part reports reduce the need for expensive post-build inspections like CT scanning.
